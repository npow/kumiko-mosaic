Left: source as fitted to the lattice. Right: the planned panel drawn with the real insert silhouettes. Every insert is a kumiko line pattern (2 mm strips, openings at least 2.5 mm, at most 65% covered) in one of 8 purchasable Bambu PLA filaments over a black background insert, in a dark frame; a cell's tone comes only from line density. At most 4 distinct patterns per panel. Shown at 40 and 60 columns; the mm size follows the pitch (50 mm here). Rule of thumb: 40 columns reads from across a room, 60 is clearly the picture. Simple, bold subjects work best.
